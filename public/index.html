How it works
Longitudinal intelligence,
not daily snapshots.

Single-day data is noise. RhythmPal builds a longitudinal model of your physiology — revealing meaningful signal across weeks and months.

Biomarker Integration

Oura, Apple Health, Garmin, Whoop, Fitbit. HRV, SpO₂, sleep stages — unified physiological timeline.

Adaptive Memory Model

Builds a continuous baseline model. Learns how your body responds to sleep, food, and stress over time.

Cross-Domain Pattern Detection

HRV suppression after late meals. Load accumulation before illness. Cross-domain signal no single metric reveals.

The Science

Built on peer-reviewed health science.

HRV as a Health Proxy

Heart rate variability is the most validated non-invasive marker of autonomic nervous system function, correlating with recovery, stress resilience, and cardiovascular health. (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2017)

Chrono-Nutrition

Meal timing relative to circadian phase significantly impacts metabolic outcomes independent of caloric intake. Late-night eating suppresses melatonin and elevates fasting glucose. (Sutton et al., Cell Metabolism, 2018)

Sleep Architecture & Recovery

Slow-wave sleep is the primary window for physical repair and memory consolidation. REM duration predicts emotional regulation and cognitive performance the following day. (Walker, 2017)
