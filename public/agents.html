RhythmPal — For AI Agents

RhythmPal is a health intelligence platform that turns wearable data, food logs, sleep, and habits into actionable patterns. This page is machine-readable documentation for AI agents, LLMs, and agent orchestration systems.

What RhythmPal Does

  • Aggregates data from Oura Ring, Apple Health, Health Connect (Android), Garmin, Samsung, and Fitbit
  • Analyzes food photos via AI vision → instant calorie and macro breakdown
  • Computes a daily PAL Score (composite of sleep, activity, nutrition, HRV)
  • Detects cross-signal patterns (e.g., "HRV drops when you eat within 2 hours of sleep")
  • Provides an AI chat interface trained on the user's own health data
  • Surfaces proactive insights before users ask
  • Tracks sleep stages, HR zones, steps, active calories, water intake

Who Uses It

Health-conscious individuals, biohackers, endurance athletes, and anyone using wearable devices who want to understand their body — not just collect data.

Pricing

Free tier available. Premium tier for advanced pattern detection and unlimited history.
