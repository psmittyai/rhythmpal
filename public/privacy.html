🔒 Privacy Statement

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Your health data is deeply personal. This statement explains exactly what RhythmPal collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We believe in radical transparency about data — no surprises, no fine print tricks.

🛡 Our Privacy Promises at a Glance

🚫
Never sold Your health data is never sold to advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.
🤖
Not used for training We do not use your personal health data to train AI models for any external purpose.
📤
Exportable You can request a full export of your data at any time.
🗑️
Deletable Delete your account and all data is permanently erased. No lingering copies.

1 What Data We Collect

Data Type What It Includes Why We Collect It
Account info Email address, display name, hashed password, account tier To identify you and manage your account
Food logs Meal descriptions, calories, macros, food photos (analyzed then discarded), timestamps To provide nutrition tracking and insights
Wearable data Sleep metrics, HRV, steps, heart rate — pulled from connected devices with your permission To surface health patterns and correlations
Chat messages Your questions and our AI responses within the app To provide context-aware AI insights and conversation history
Usage data Feature usage, session timestamps, error logs To improve the service and diagnose issues

Food photos: When you snap a meal photo, it is sent to our AI provider for analysis and immediately discarded. We do not store your food photos on our servers.

2 How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Provide the RhythmPal service to you — logging, insights, AI chat
  • Generate your health patterns, correlations, and weekly/monthly reports
  • Send you notifications you've opted into (daily summaries, insights alerts)
  • Improve the RhythmPal product based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Process payments through Stripe (we never see your full card number)
✅ We will never use your personal health data for advertising targeting, sell it to insurance companies, share it with employers, or use it for any purpose other than delivering RhythmPal to you.

3 AI & Third-Party Services

RhythmPal uses AI to analyze your health patterns and answer your questions. Here's how that works:

  • Food photo analysis: Images are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Anthropic's API terms prohibit using API inputs for model training. Images are not stored by us after analysis.
  • AI chat: Your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API with relevant context from your health data to generate responses. Your health data is treated as confidential under Anthropic's API terms.
  • Payments: Stripe processes all payments. We do not store card details. See Stripe's privacy policy for how they handle payment data.

We carefully vet all third-party services. We do not share your data with analytics companies, ad networks, or data brokers.

4 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Here's our retention policy:

  • Active accounts: All data retained indefinitely (Free: 7-day rolling history; Premium: unlimited)
  • Cancelled Premium: Account downgraded to Free tier; data retained under Free tier limits
  • Deleted accounts: All data permanently erased within 24 hours of deletion request
  • Anonymized analytics: Aggregate usage stats retained indefinitely (no personal identifiers)

We do not keep backups of deleted account data beyond our standard 24-hour backup rotation window.

5 Your Rights

You have the following rights over your data:

  • Access: Request a complete export of all your RhythmPal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your personal information via account settings
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data permanently via Profile → Settings → Danger Zone
  • Portability: Export your data in a standard format (CSV/JSON) — contact us to request
  • Objection: Opt out of any non-essential data processing — contact us

Users in the EU/EEA have additional rights under GDPR. Users in California have rights under CCPA. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

6 Security

We take security seriously. Our measures include:

  • All data encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
  • Authentication via signed JWT tokens with expiry
  • API keys stored in environment variables, never in code
  • Database access restricted to application layer only
  • Regular dependency audits and security updates

No system is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to security@rhythmpal.com.

7 Children's Privacy

RhythmPal is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately at privacy@rhythmpal.com and we will delete the account.

8 Changes to This Statement

We may update this Privacy Statement as our practices evolve. When we make material changes, we'll notify you via email and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Continuing to use RhythmPal after a privacy update constitutes acceptance of the updated statement. If you disagree with changes, you may delete your account before they take effect.
